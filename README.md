An independent, beginner-friendly reference to the U.S.-market 2019 Toyota Mirai factory Entune 3.0/JBL system, ordinary phone connectivity, historical community-reported CarPlay research, and California's hydrogen network.

## Scope of this project

- Factory radio, navigation, and JBL audio overview
- Physical audio controls and ordinary Bluetooth, USB, and AUX use
- Historical documentation of a community-reported CarPlay experiment
- Clear separation between Toyota documentation, community claims, and owner-reported experience
- Links to official hydrogen station and California agency resources

This repository is an informational research reference. It is deliberately **not** a firmware repository, download mirror, installation tutorial, repair manual, or technical service instruction.

## Factory system

The guide focuses on the original U.S.-market 2019 Mirai Entune 3.0 Premium Audio system and factory JBL equipment. Equipment can vary by market or vehicle configuration; consult the actual vehicle's documentation and Toyota resources.

Toyota's 2019 U.S. Mirai materials referenced by this project do not list Apple CarPlay as a factory feature. Bluetooth phone/audio and other media inputs are part of the original system. A retrofit, interface module, or replacement radio is a separate aftermarket or community matter.

## Apple CarPlay: historical community research only

> **Important: this repository does not provide a CarPlay installation procedure.**
>
> The Reddit discussion [“Getting Apple Carplay on 2018 Toyota Mirai (How-To)”](https://www.reddit.com/r/Mirai/comments/1705d2k/) (October 5, 2023) describes adapting Camry multimedia software packages and reports applicability to 2018–2020 Mirai vehicles. The Mirai-specific compatibility statement is the Reddit contributor's claim—not Toyota confirmation.
>
> Toyota T-SB-0057-19 lists 2018 Camry, Camry Hybrid, and Sienna applications; it does **not** list Mirai. The bulletin warns that incorrect execution may damage the head unit. [Read the Toyota bulletin hosted by NHTSA](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2019/MC-10161540-9999.pdf).
>
> Incompatible software or procedures may disable or damage a multimedia unit. Recovery may be costly or unavailable. This project does not recommend attempting the modification and does not promise compatibility, functionality, recovery, or warranty coverage. Consult Toyota or a qualified automotive multimedia specialist about the exact head-unit part number and vehicle.

The Reddit post identifies two Camry package labels: T-SB-0057-19 Audio Plus/Premium and 2018 Camry Premium Audio. They are recorded here only to describe the public discussion. They are not Mirai-specific Toyota firmware recommendations. This repository does not host, distribute, mirror, validate, or link to firmware files, and does not provide USB preparation or installation steps.

The project owner reports that a modification has functioned on their personal vehicle for more than three years. This is one owner's account of one vehicle and configuration, not independently verified evidence, a guarantee, or a recommendation for anyone else.

## California hydrogen network

Use these external sources for current station information, new projects, permitting/development progress, and industry updates:

| Resource | What it provides | Published cadence |
|---|---|---|
| [H2FCP live station map](https://h2fcp.org/stationmap) | Station locations and station-status information for drivers | Check the map and each station's status/timestamp |
| [H2FCP Resources](https://h2fcp.org/resources) | H2 Stations List, including projects in development | H2FCP says the development list is updated monthly |
| [H2FCP News](https://h2fcp.org/news) | Hydrogen/fuel-cell news, announcements, and events | As published |
| [CARB hydrogen infrastructure program](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/hydrogen-fueling-infrastructure/about) | California policy, funding, infrastructure information, and evaluations | Annual evaluations |
| [California Energy Commission hydrogen dashboard](https://www.energy.ca.gov/data-reports/energy-almanac/zero-emission-vehicle-and-infrastructure-statistics-collection/hydrogen) | Statewide station counts and planned project data | CEC says quarterly |

Proposed, permitting, construction, and commissioning are development stages—not confirmation that a station is open and ready to fuel. Check station status before driving.
