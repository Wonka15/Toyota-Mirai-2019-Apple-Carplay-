Start with the factory system

The U.S.-market 2019 Mirai came with Toyota Entune 3.0 Premium Audio and JBL equipment. This guide focuses on that original system—not newer multimedia units found in later Mirai model years.

🎵

Premium JBL audio

The 2019 brochure lists an 11-speaker JBL system, including a subwoofer and amplifier.

📻

Radio & media

AM/FM, HD Radio, SiriusXM capability, CD playback, USB media, AUX input, and Bluetooth audio.

🧭

Entune navigation

The original package included a 7-inch touchscreen and Dynamic Navigation features.

Important CarPlay distinction: Apple CarPlay was not listed as a factory feature for the U.S. 2019 Mirai. The original system uses Entune 3.0. A CarPlay retrofit or replacement is a separate modification; compatibility depends on the hardware and installation method. Do not assume a software update alone will add CarPlay.

Connect your phone

The original 2019 system supports Bluetooth phone functions and Bluetooth audio. USB and AUX are additional ways to connect compatible devices.

📱

Bluetooth calling

Register a compatible phone through connectivity settings. Confirm pairing on both the car display and phone, then allow contacts access if desired.

🎧

Bluetooth music

Select Bluetooth audio as the source after pairing. Start playback on your phone and use the car's audio controls for supported functions.

🔌

USB & AUX

Use the media USB port for supported USB audio devices, or the AUX input for compatible analog audio. Some USB ports are charge-only.

Pairing tip: Park before setting up a phone. Remove old pairings if the device list is full, keep Bluetooth enabled, and check that the phone is not already connected to another vehicle.

Apple CarPlay on a 2019 Mirai

A community-reported wired CarPlay method exists for certain 2018–2020 Mirai vehicles. It is not a Toyota-issued Mirai update.

Original community source: Reddit post “Getting Apple Carplay on 2018 Toyota Mirai (How-To),” October 5, 2023. The poster describes using T-SB-0057-19 Camry audio packages and reports the method for 2018, 2019, and 2020 Mirai. The Mirai-specific compatibility claim comes from the Reddit poster, not Toyota. Read the original Reddit discussion ↗.

What the community post says you need

The Reddit guide refers to two Toyota Camry multimedia software packages:

  1. T-SB-0057-19 — Camry Audio Plus / Premium
  2. 2018 Camry Premium Audio

These are the two package names described by the community post—not Mirai-specific Toyota update files. The exact archive names and contents should be checked against the original source before anyone uses them. This page does not host or redistribute firmware.

USB preparation reported by the community

The Reddit instructions specify a USB flash drive formatted as FAT32 (not exFAT). The post recommends a 16 GB USB drive. After extracting the package, the post says the update files should be at the USB drive's top level (root), not buried inside an extra nested folder. A wrong folder structure can cause the update to fail or damage the head unit.

Important: FAT32 and a correct folder layout do not establish that the files are compatible with your Mirai. Confirm the exact head-unit model ID and package applicability first. Do not proceed based only on the vehicle year or a Camry label.

Important: Toyota T-SB-0057-19 is a Phase 3 Panasonic bulletin listing 2018 Camry, Camry Hybrid, and Sienna—not Mirai. It describes a full multimedia reformat and explicitly warns that incorrect execution may damage the head unit. Do not assume the bulletin authorizes or guarantees this Mirai modification.
Factory configuration

Original Entune 3.0

CarPlay is not shown as a factory feature in Toyota's 2019 U.S. Mirai brochure. Bluetooth and Entune connectivity are the original phone options.

Aftermarket route

Replacement head unit

A compatible replacement may add wired or wireless CarPlay, but fitment, wiring, JBL amplifier integration, camera, steering-wheel controls, and vehicle settings must be checked.

Research first

Interface / retrofit adapter

Some vehicles use interface modules, but do not buy one based on a generic Toyota listing. Confirm exact 2019 Mirai compatibility in writing.

This is the front USB port used for wired CarPlay and the wireless adapter. The rear console USB sockets are charging ports—not the CarPlay data connection.

⇄

Optional: make the CarPlay connection wireless

The 2019 Mirai has a USB media/data port in the front center console. This is the port used for the wired CarPlay connection—not the rear charging ports. After the community software modification has successfully enabled wired CarPlay, a small wireless CarPlay USB adapter may let the iPhone connect to CarPlay over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi instead of plugging the phone into the car every trip.

  1. Leave the wireless adapter plugged into the front center-console USB media/data port.
  2. Pair the iPhone with the adapter following its own instructions.
  3. After setup, the phone connects wirelessly when you start the car (adapter behavior varies).

What this does—and does not do: The adapter stays plugged into the vehicle's USB port, so it does not eliminate use of that port. It reduces repeated plugging and unplugging of the iPhone cable, which may reduce wear on the phone cable and USB socket. It does not add CarPlay by itself.

Compatibility is unconfirmed for this specific Mirai retrofit. A typical wireless CarPlay dongle requires a working wired CarPlay system first. Test wired CarPlay directly in the center-console USB media port after the software modification, then ask the adapter maker to confirm compatibility with the exact 2019 Mirai head unit and its modified software before buying. Do not assume a generic “Toyota 2014–2019” listing covers the Mirai.

Use the front center-console USB media/data port for this purpose. The rear USB ports are charging ports and are not the CarPlay data connection.

Before anyone considers this community method

  • Identify the exact head-unit model ID and software version in the vehicle’s Software Update / Software Information screen.
  • Confirm the installed head-unit manufacturer; the method is not universal across all radio hardware.
  • Do not use files merely because they are labelled Toyota or Camry. Verify exact model ID and package compatibility.
  • Use the Reddit post and Toyota bulletin as research references, not as a guarantee or official Mirai procedure.
  • Never interrupt an installation; only follow instructions verified for the exact unit.
