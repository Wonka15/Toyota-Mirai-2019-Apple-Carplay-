Get to know your Mirai radio.

A practical guide to the factory Entune 3.0 audio system, everyday radio controls, phone connections, and what to know before adding Apple CarPlay.

2019 Toyota MiraiEntune 3.0 Premium AudioJBL sound system

Start with the factory system

The U.S.-market 2019 Mirai came with Toyota Entune 3.0 Premium Audio and JBL equipment. This guide focuses on that original system—not newer multimedia units found in later Mirai model years.

🎵

Premium JBL audio

The 2019 brochure lists an 11-speaker JBL system, including a subwoofer and amplifier.

📻

Radio & media

AM/FM, HD Radio, SiriusXM capability, CD playback, USB media, AUX input, and Bluetooth audio.

🧭

Entune navigation

The original package included a 7-inch touchscreen and Dynamic Navigation features.

Important CarPlay distinction: Apple CarPlay was not listed as a factory feature for the U.S. 2019 Mirai. The original system uses Entune 3.0. A CarPlay retrofit or replacement is a separate modification; compatibility depends on the hardware and installation method. Do not assume a software update alone will add CarPlay.

Connect your phone

The original 2019 system supports Bluetooth phone functions and Bluetooth audio. USB and AUX are additional ways to connect compatible devices.

📱

Bluetooth calling

Register a compatible phone through connectivity settings. Confirm pairing on both the car display and phone, then allow contacts access if desired.

🎧

Bluetooth music

Select Bluetooth audio as the source after pairing. Start playback on your phone and use the car's audio controls for supported functions.

🔌

USB & AUX

Use the media USB port for supported USB audio devices, or the AUX input for compatible analog audio. Some USB ports are charge-only.

Pairing tip: Park before setting up a phone. Remove old pairings if the device list is full, keep Bluetooth enabled, and check that the phone is not already connected to another vehicle.

Apple CarPlay on a 2019 Mirai

If you want Apple Maps, messages, podcasts, and iPhone apps on the factory screen, first identify exactly which radio/head unit is installed.

Factory configuration

Original Entune 3.0

CarPlay is not shown as a factory feature in Toyota's 2019 U.S. Mirai brochure. Bluetooth and Entune connectivity are the original phone options.

Aftermarket route

Replacement head unit

A compatible replacement may add wired or wireless CarPlay, but fitment, wiring, JBL amplifier integration, camera, steering-wheel controls, and vehicle settings must be checked.

Research first

Interface / retrofit adapter

Some vehicles use interface modules, but do not buy one based on a generic Toyota listing. Confirm exact 2019 Mirai compatibility in writing.

Before buying parts: verify the radio part number, connector photos, JBL amplifier configuration, and whether the kit retains the backup camera and steering-wheel controls. Ask the installer how factory functions will be preserved. Never cut or probe wiring around high-voltage fuel-cell components.

What to document for a reliable fitment guide

  • Photo of the radio screen and dashboard
  • Radio model / Toyota part number from the unit label, if safely accessible
  • Whether the car has the factory JBL audio system
  • Connector and harness photos from a qualified installer
  • Exact CarPlay interface or head-unit model and confirmed vehicle compatibility
