Start with the factory system

The U.S.-market 2019 Mirai came with Toyota Entune 3.0 Premium Audio and JBL equipment. This guide focuses on that original system—not newer multimedia units found in later Mirai model years.

🎵

Premium JBL audio

The 2019 brochure lists an 11-speaker JBL system, including a subwoofer and amplifier.

📻

Radio & media

AM/FM, HD Radio, SiriusXM capability, CD playback, USB media, AUX input, and Bluetooth audio.

🧭

Entune navigation

The original package included a 7-inch touchscreen and Dynamic Navigation features.

Important CarPlay distinction: Apple CarPlay was not listed as a factory feature for the U.S. 2019 Mirai. The original system uses Entune 3.0. A CarPlay retrofit or replacement is a separate modification; compatibility depends on the hardware and installation method. Do not assume a software update alone will add CarPlay.

Connect your phone

The original 2019 system supports Bluetooth phone functions and Bluetooth audio. USB and AUX are additional ways to connect compatible devices.

📱

Bluetooth calling

Register a compatible phone through connectivity settings. Confirm pairing on both the car display and phone, then allow contacts access if desired.

🎧

Bluetooth music

Select Bluetooth audio as the source after pairing. Start playback on your phone and use the car's audio controls for supported functions.

🔌

USB & AUX

Use the media USB port for supported USB audio devices, or the AUX input for compatible analog audio. Some USB ports are charge-only.

Pairing tip: Park before setting up a phone. Remove old pairings if the device list is full, keep Bluetooth enabled, and check that the phone is not already connected to another vehicle.

Is Entune still a thing?

Yes—in the sense that Entune 3.0 is the name of the factory multimedia platform fitted to certain older Toyota vehicles, including the system discussed on this page. But the name does not mean every original online service still works. Toyota states that Entune App Suite services were discontinued after October 30, 2023. Depending on vehicle and service, app icons may remain visible but be unavailable. Ordinary head-unit functions such as radio, Bluetooth calling/audio, USB media, and the built-in navigation system are separate and may continue to work.

Toyota's U.S. support page also explains that certain older Mirai connected features were affected by the earlier 3G network shutdown; that notice identifies 2016–2017 Mirai models, so it should not be automatically applied to the 2019 model. Check Toyota's information for the exact model year and feature.
